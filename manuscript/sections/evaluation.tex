\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:evaluation}


Our evaluation is organized around four research questions: how accurately ReCall recovers functions across applications, bundlers, and libraries (RQ1); how neighborhood ranking and module-aware graph construction contribute to recovery quality and efficiency (RQ2); how the POG threshold and validation model affect the tradeoff between recovery quality and computational cost (RQ3); and how ReCall can support practical analysis tasks (RQ4). We first describe the dataset and ground-truth construction in Section~\ref{sec:data-collection}, then discuss the four research questions in Sections~\ref{sec:rq1}--\ref{sec:rq4}, respectively.
